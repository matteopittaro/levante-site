Frequently asked questions

Do my trips sync if I change iPhone?

Yes, as long as iCloud is on with the same account on both. Syncing happens on its own, in the background too.

Where does my data end up?

On the device, and with iCloud on, in your own private iCloud space. There is no server behind the app and nobody can read your content. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can I use the app without iCloud?

Yes. Without iCloud everything works as usual, but the data stays only on the device where it was entered.

How do I share a trip with someone?

Open the trip and pull the page down to reveal the top bar: the share icon offers «Add people». The invitation goes out through the system sharing panel, the same one used for photos or notes. The same command, which then reads «Manage people», shows who has already accepted and can remove someone at any time.

What does the person I invite see?

Everything in the trip: places, accommodation, tickets, checklists, photos, notes and expenses, except the checklists marked «Just for me», which stay yours even after sharing.

If I remove someone, or leave a shared trip, does the data disappear?

If you own the trip and delete it, it disappears for everyone. If you remove a participant, they lose access and the trip stays with you. If instead you leave a trip someone else shared with you, it disappears only from your device: the owner and the other participants lose nothing.

The weather forecast is not showing

Weather needs an internet connection, and it is shown for dates within the range the forecast service covers. For trips far off in the future it may not be available.

I deleted a trip by mistake

Deleting removes the places, accommodation, checklists and photos attached to it, and it cannot be undone. With iCloud on, the deletion reaches your other devices too.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes, and it is built for it: a trip already downloaded can be read and edited on a plane or anywhere with no signal. Changes made offline sync on their own once the signal is back, across every device, even when the people travelling with you have been editing too.

Why do the day's times keep running into the night?

Because no accommodation covers that night, and without one the app does not know where you stop: it keeps working out the journey into the next day. This is deliberate, since some nights are spent travelling, on a ferry or a flight. If you are sleeping somewhere, adding the accommodation closes the day there. A place marked «Back to the room», put last in the day, does the same.

How do I change the language or the currency?

In Settings › Language & currency. With no choice made, the phone's own settings apply. The currency is the one used for trips created here and the one the accounts are read in, including those received from other people: amounts stay recorded in the trip's own currency, only the reading changes.

I recorded an expense in another currency: which rate is used?

The rate of the day it was recorded, and from then on it is frozen: the accounts never shift with the market. An expense recorded with no connection waits and converts itself as soon as the rates arrive. The rates come from a public list, and nothing of yours is sent to get them.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only when you ask for it: by tapping the location button on a trip's full map, or on the day's map in «Today». It shows where you are in relation to the places and orients the compass. You can refuse the permission and everything else keeps working. The location is not stored and is not sent anywhere.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature

Best from inside the app: Settings › Support › «Report a problem» prepares an email with a technical report attached — version, sync status, recent events — which makes the report far more useful. The report is visible in full before sending, and goes only if you send it. For an idea there is «Suggest a feature», which attaches nothing: only the text you write. Writing to the address above works just as well, saying what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
