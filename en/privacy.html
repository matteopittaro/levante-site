Levante

Privacy policy

In force from 17 September 2026

Italiano · English

In short: Levante does not collect your data. Everything written in the app stays on your device and, with iCloud on, in your own private iCloud space. The developer cannot read it.

What we never do:

  • We have no server of our own where your data ends up: it exists nowhere outside your device and your iCloud
  • We do not ask you to create an account, nor a user profile
  • We do not track your behaviour and we use no analytics tools
  • We show no advertising and share no data with ad networks or other third parties for marketing
  • We do not sell or hand over your data to anyone, in any form

Who handles the data

The data controller is Matteo Pittaro, developer of the Levante app. For any question about this policy, write to supporto.levante@icloud.com.

What the app handles

Levante lets you create trips, places, checklists and notes, and attach photos and documents. All of this content is created by you and stays under your control:

  • Trips, places, checklists and notes — saved in the app's local store on the device
  • Photos and documents — saved on the device; any text recognised in an image is extracted on the device itself
  • Coordinates of destinations — the places you pick when creating a trip or adding a place
  • Your name — the one written in Settings: it lets the other participants recognise you in shared trips, and stays on your device and in your iCloud
  • App preferences — settings saved locally

Where this data ends up

The data stays in the app's local store. With iCloud on, it is synced to your own private iCloud database so you find it again on your devices. That database is accessible only to you: the developer has no way of reading its contents.

There is no server run by the developer. There are no accounts to create, and no user profiles.

Reporting problems

When you use «Report a problem» in Settings, the app prepares an email for you with a technical report attached: app version, iCloud sync status, recent sync events and some lines of the sharing log, which may include trip or participant names. The report is visible in full before sending, in your mail app: no data leaves the device automatically, it goes only if you choose to send it.

Using «Suggest a feature» attaches no technical report: only the text you write.

Sharing with other people

Levante lets you prepare a trip together with someone else through the system sharing panel. From that moment the places, checklists, photos, notes and expenses of that trip are visible to every participant you invited, and the reverse holds too: you see what they have written or uploaded. Participants also see each other's identity (name and Apple account), shown by the system panel as it would be for a shared photo album; the developer does not receive it.

One deliberate exception: checklists marked «Just for me» stay on your device and in your private iCloud even inside a shared trip. They are never sent to the other participants, not even by mistake.

If you delete a trip you own, it disappears for whoever received it too. If instead you leave a trip someone else shared with you, it disappears only from your device: their data stays intact for everyone else.

External services

Maps, place search and route calculation are Apple's. When you search for a destination, the text you type and the map area you are looking at reach Apple, which handles them under its own privacy policy. Your name and the contents of your trips are never sent there. The same holds for iCloud, which keeps your synced data without the developer being able to read it.

To show you the weather forecast for a destination, the app queries the Open-Meteo service. The request contains only the coordinates of the place you chose and the dates of the trip.

That request contains no name, no email address, no device or session identifier, and nothing else that could lead back to you. The coordinates are those of the place you want to go to, not of the place where you are.

To convert expenses recorded in a currency other than the trip's, the app downloads exchange rates from open.er-api.com and, as a fallback, from the European Central Bank. It is a read-only request: it asks for the day's list and nothing more.

Nothing of yours is sent to them: not your expenses, not their amounts, not which currencies you use. The conversion happens on the device, after the list has arrived.

These, Apple included, are the only services the app talks to, and none of them receives the contents of your trips or anything that could lead back to you. The developer shares no personal data with third parties, for the simplest of reasons: there is none to share. The app relies only on services that give the data they receive protection equal to the one described on this page, and there are no parent, subsidiary or otherwise related companies with access to your data.

Device location

The places of trips and days are entered by you through search: for those the app does not use geolocation.

Your location is requested only when you ask for it: by tapping the location button on a trip's full map, or on the day's map in «Today». It shows where you are in relation to the places and orients the compass. The permission is the «while using the app» kind, you can refuse it, and everything else keeps working. The location is only used to draw the map on the device: it is not stored, not synced to iCloud and not sent to anyone, the developer included.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera access only when you choose to photograph a ticket or a document, and for photo library access only to save the pictures you take there. Text recognition in images happens entirely on the device: no image is sent anywhere to be analysed.

Notifications

Levante sends no promotional notifications and does not ask permission to show you alerts. The app does receive silent notifications from iCloud: technical messages Apple delivers when you change your data on another device, whose only job is to keep syncing up to date even when the app is not in the foreground. They contain none of your content and produce no alert on screen.

Tracking and advertising

Levante contains no tracking systems, no behavioural analytics and no advertising. It shares no data with third parties for marketing and takes part in no ad networks.

Keeping and deleting

Data stays on the device until you delete it. You can delete individual trips from the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it. Content synced to iCloud is managed from the device's iCloud settings.

There is no retention period and nothing kept by the developer: with no server, no copy of your data exists outside your device and your iCloud, so there is no deletion to request from anyone. The only exception is a report email you chose to send: asking at the address above is enough to have it deleted.

The permissions you granted, location, camera and photo library, can be withdrawn at any time in iOS Settings › Levante, and the app keeps working without them. Syncing is turned off from there too, in the iCloud settings: the data then stays on that device alone.

Your rights

The European data protection regulation (GDPR) gives you the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing and receive it in a readable format. For your own trips you exercise these rights directly in the app, without contacting anyone: the data never leaves your device and your personal iCloud space.

The same holds for a shared trip, with one natural limit: you can always delete or correct what you wrote, but not what another person wrote in the same trip. That is their data, not yours, and their rights over it remain theirs.

If you still believe your data is not being handled lawfully, you can contact your national data protection authority.

Children

Levante is not aimed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Changes to this policy

If the app should ever handle data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change accordingly.
